# Riftbound: League of Legends TCG — How to Play

## Goal of the Game

Riftbound is a Trading Card Game for 2-4 players where you join forces with
your favorite League of Legends champions. Play out your army and conquer
the battlefields of Runeterra to win.

Each battlefield you take with your units scores 1 point, and each turn you
hold one earns you 1 more. The first to 8 points wins!

## Parts of a Deck

To play Riftbound, you'll need a deck. Preconstructed decks are a good
place to start! Your deck has a number of components:

- **1 Champion Legend**: This special card determines what champion you're
  teaming up with and which Domains you can play in your deck. It starts in
  your Legend Zone.
- **Battlefields**: These horizontally laid out cards represent locations
  on Runeterra that you and your opponents will fight over. Set them aside
  for now.
- **12 Rune Deck cards**: These are the runes you'll use to pay costs during
  the game. You need exactly 12 runes, in any combination of the Domains of
  your champion legend. (These decks use an even split of 6 and 6, which is
  always a good place to start.)
- **40 Main Deck cards**: These are the units and spells that you'll play
  during the game. One of these is your Chosen Champion, which goes to your
  Champion Zone alongside your legend. Shuffle the remaining 39 cards to
  become your starting Main Deck.

## Chosen Champion

Your Chosen Champion is a champion unit that matches your Champion Legend.
It starts in your Main Deck, but is put in the Champion Zone before the
game. You can choose any Champion with the name indicated on your Champion
Legend. So if your legend is Loose Cannon, you can select either Jinx,
Rebel or Jinx, Demolitionist as your Chosen Champion.

Your Main Deck can have a maximum of 3 copies of each card, including your
Chosen Champion. So you could have 2 more copies of your Chosen Champion in
your deck, plus up to 3 copies of any other card your legend allows —
including other cards that represent the same character.

You can also include up to 3 copies of a signature card that matches your
Champion Legend — usually a signature spell like Super Mega Death Rocket!
Signature cards can only be included with their matching Legend.

## Setting Up the Game

All players put their battlefields in the Battlefield Zone. Then each
player sets up their board: Champion Legend and Chosen Champion face up to
one side; shuffle the remaining 39 Main Deck cards; shuffle the Rune Deck
separately. Leave open space for your Base, your Runes, and your Trash.

## Starting the Game

Determine the starting player by shuffling the selected battlefields and
choosing one at random — that battlefield's owner goes first. With 4
players, remove the battlefield owned by the player going first, so there
are only 3 battlefields.

Each player draws 4 cards. Before the game begins, each player may take up
to one mulligan: choose up to 2 cards to recycle (bottom of deck), then
draw that many.

The last player plays an extra rune on their first turn. With 4 players,
the first player also skips drawing a card on their first turn. Turns
proceed clockwise.

### Terms to Know

- **Exhaust**: Turn a card sideways, usually to pay a cost.
- **Ready**: Turn a card upright; the opposite of exhaust.
- **Recycle**: Take a card from its current location and put it on the
  bottom of the appropriate deck.
- **Channel**: Take the top card(s) of your Rune Deck and put them on the
  board. You channel 2 runes at the start of each turn by default.
- **Move**: Units can exhaust to move to/from battlefields. Units with
  Ganking can move directly from one battlefield to another.
- **Card**: In rules text, "card" means a card from your Main Deck — not
  legends, runes, battlefields, or tokens.
- **Tokens**: Units created during play instead of starting in your deck.

## Playing Cards

Costs have two components:
- **Energy cost** (a number): runes you must exhaust to pay.
- **Power cost** (symbols): runes you must recycle, one per symbol, of the
  matching Domain. You can recycle exhausted runes to pay this, including
  ones exhausted for the Energy cost.

## Types of Main Deck Cards

- **Units**: played exhausted, to your base or a battlefield you control. A
  unit with damage ≥ its Might is killed.
- **Spells**: resolve their text, then go to the trash. Never sit on the
  board.
- **Gear**: played readied to your base. Don't move/attack, don't have
  Might, don't take damage, don't fight in combat.

### Timing of Playing Cards and Abilities

By default, cards/abilities can only be played on your turn with no combat
occurring and nothing else being played. `Action` cards can also be played
during showdowns, including combat. `Reaction` cards can be played on your
turn, during showdowns, or before another spell/ability resolves (the
Reaction resolves first). Abilities that Add Energy/Power happen
immediately and can't be reacted to.

## Steps of a Turn

1. **Awaken Phase**: Ready your exhausted units, legend, runes.
2. **Beginning Phase**: Resolve "start of turn" abilities, then score a
   point for each battlefield you control (Holding).
3. **Channel Phase**: Channel the top two runes from your Rune Deck.
4. **Draw Phase**: Draw 1. No max hand size.
5. **Action Phase**: Play cards, use abilities, move units — in any order,
   as many times as able. Ends when you choose to stop.

At end of turn: "end of turn" abilities happen, all units heal to full.

## Moving, Conquering & Control

To score, conquer battlefields by moving units there. Once conquered, you
control it as long as you have ≥1 unit there. If a battlefield is
unoccupied, moving a unit there conquers it immediately.

**Combat**: moving units to a battlefield with an enemy starts combat.
Resolve "When I defend" then "When I attack" abilities first (either
player may use Reactions). Then it's a Showdown — players may play
actions/reactions hidden there, with Reactions resolving before the spell
or ability that prompted them.

In a 3+ player game, only two players can have units in a combat at once.
By default only those two can play Actions/Reactions during it, but either
can invite others in.

## Fight!

Each player sums the Might of their units present and deals that much
damage, distributed as they choose, among the enemy units present. Lethal
damage must be assigned to one unit fully before assigning to another;
units with Tank must be assigned damage first.

All damage is simultaneous. Units with damage ≥ Might are killed. All units
everywhere then heal. Damage doesn't reduce Might (a 6-Might unit with 5
damage still deals 6 in combat).

If only attackers remain, the attacker conquers and scores 1 point. If
defenders remain, no conquer occurs and surviving attackers return to
base.

**Winning the Game**: first to 8 points (11 for a team). The winning point
specifically must come from Holding a battlefield, or from scoring (hold or
conquer) all battlefields in the same turn — otherwise you draw a card
instead of scoring.

## Modes of Play

- **Duel**: 2 players, 8 points, default rules above.
- **Match**: Duel best-of-3, with battlefield selection instead of random.
- **Skirmish**: 3-player free-for-all, 3 battlefields, first player skips
  their first draw.
- **War**: 4-player free-for-all, similar to Skirmish plus the first player
  removes their starting battlefield.
- **2v2 Teams**: combined 11 points. Allies don't share cards/resources/
  control, but their units/cards are "friendly" to each other.

## Additional Rules — Keyword Glossary

- **Accelerate**: for a cost, enters the board ready.
- **Legion**: trigger an effect if you've already played another Main Deck
  card this turn.
- **Assault**: additional Might while attacking.
- **Shield**: additional Might while defending.
- **Tank**: takes combat damage first.
- **Deathknell**: triggers when this unit dies.
- **Deflect**: opponents pay additional Power to target this card.
- **Ganking**: may move directly from one battlefield to another.
- **Temporary**: killed at the start of your turn.
- **Vision**: look at the top card of your Main Deck when played; may
  recycle it.
- **Hidden**: during your turn, hide a card facedown at a battlefield you
  control by spending a Power of any type (one hidden card per
  battlefield). Stays as long as you control that battlefield; can be
  revealed and played as a Reaction for 0, targeting only that battlefield.
- **Stun**: a stunned unit doesn't deal damage in combat this turn.
- **Buff**: a marker giving +1 Might. Max one buff per unit by default;
  some cards let you spend a buff for an effect.
